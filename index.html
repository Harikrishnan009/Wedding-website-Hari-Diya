How We Met

It started with a simple glance, a shared smile, and a spark that set our souls on fire. Two individual paths crossing at just the right time, weaving into a single, beautiful journey. What felt like a coincidental meeting soon revealed itself to be a pre-destined union, an answered prayer in the quiet moments of 11:11.

The Proposal

Under a canopy of stars, with whispers of promises and eyes filled with tears of joy, we chose forever. It wasn't just a question; it was the alignment of our hopes, dreams, and futures. A commitment to hold hands through life's storms and dance together in its sunshine.
